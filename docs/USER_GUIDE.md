# HostelGuard: Student & Warden User Guide

Welcome to **HostelGuard**, the official digital attendance and residential safety portal.

---

## 1. Student User Guide

### 1.1. Signing In
1. Navigate to the HostelGuard portal URL on your mobile phone or laptop.
2. Enter your **Roll Number** (or registered email/phone) and **Password**.
3. Tap **Sign In**.
4. *Forgot Password?* Tap the **Forgot?** button to receive a 6-digit verification code via SMS or Email to reset your password immediately.

---

### 1.2. Marking Evening Roll Call Attendance
Roll-call attendance is required every evening during the scheduled attendance window (normally **7:00 PM – 7:30 PM**).

```
Step 1: Location  ➔  Step 2: Identity  ➔  Step 3: Verification  ➔  Step 4: Complete
```

1. **Step 1: Location**:
   * Open the **Mark Attendance** tab.
   * Tap the **✓ Mark Attendance** button.
   * Your device verifies that you are physically inside the authorized hostel boundary.
   * *Status message: "Checking your location..." ➔ "Location verified."*
   * *Tip: Ensure your phone's Wi-Fi or location services are turned on for fast positioning.*
2. **Step 2: Identity**:
   * The live camera viewfinder opens with a green oval guide.
   * Look directly at the camera in good lighting.
   * *Status message: "Look at the camera."*
3. **Step 3: Verification**:
   * Tap **✓ Verify Identity & Confirm Attendance**.
   * *Status message: "Verifying your identity..."*
4. **Step 4: Complete**:
   * You will see the confirmation screen with:
     * Status message: *"Attendance marked successfully."*
     * Date, exact time recorded, and verified identity status.

---

### 1.3. What If Attendance Is Closed or It's a Holiday?
* If you open the portal outside attendance hours, you will see a notice stating when roll call opens next (e.g. *"Attendance Opens at 7:00 PM"*).
* On official campus holidays or special exemptions, an informative banner will confirm: *"Today is a Campus Holiday: Attendance Exempt"*.

---

### 1.4. Checking Attendance History & Filing Appeals
* **My Attendance History**: View past check-in records, time stamps, and attendance percentages.
* **Submit Attendance Appeal**: If you were absent due to an official college event or medical reason:
  1. Open the **Submit Correction** tab.
  2. Select the date and write your justification reason.
  3. Tap **Submit Appeal to Warden**. Your warden will review and approve valid requests.
* **Support & Feedback**: Use this tab to submit room maintenance grievances, Wi-Fi requests, or general hostel inquiries.

---

## 2. Warden User Guide

### 2.1. Warden Overview Panel
When wardens log in, they see a focused dashboard specific to their assigned hostel block:
* **Total Residents**: Enrolled students in your block.
* **Present Today**: Residents who have verified evening roll call.
* **Absent Today**: Missing or pending residents.
* **Pending Appeals**: Outstanding correction requests awaiting review.

---

### 2.2. Tracking Absentees & Emergency Calling
1. Open the **Absence & Exceptions** section during evening roll call.
2. The list updates in real time to show students who have not yet checked in.
3. Tap the student's **Phone Number** to directly call them or contact emergency guardians to verify their whereabouts and ensure student safety.

---

### 2.3. Reviewing Student Correction Appeals
1. Open the **Correction Requests** tab.
2. Review the student's name, room number, date of absence, and documented reason.
3. Select **Approve** or **Reject**, enter an administrative review note, and submit.
4. Approved appeals automatically update the student's attendance record from `ABSENT` to `EXCUSED` or `PRESENT`.
